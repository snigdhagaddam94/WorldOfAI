4. **GTM gap: lead with honesty + speed.** "My pipeline domain has been healthcare and logistics — I pick up new domains fast. I'd spend the first 60 days embedded with RevOps and Marketing to map the data landscape before defining the roadmap."
5. **Anchor to Anthropic's mission.** Alignment with AI safety isn't just a checkbox — it's a differentiator. Articulate genuine interest in the mission.

### "If I Get Downleveled" Plan

If Anthropic offers an IC Staff/Principal DE instead of Manager:
