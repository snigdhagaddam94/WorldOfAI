### Strategy: "Sell Senior Without Lying"

**Acknowledge the stretch directly in the cover letter:** The ML research context gap is real. Position willingness to learn ML workflows rapidly with the data engineering fundamentals already at senior level.

If interviewed and the team signals an IC-track (Staff/Principal Data Engineer) role rather than EM:
- Accept if comp is still in the $300K+ range (still 2x target)
- Push for clear path to EM or TLM in 12-18 months
- Get explicit criteria: "What would EM look like and what would it take?"
