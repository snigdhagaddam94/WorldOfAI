2. **Compensation ceiling:** SWE II's top band ($183K CAD) barely reaches Vinay's floor, and the expected offer to a SWE II would land mid-band (~$155–165K CAD)

- Emphasize fast ramp: prior platform engineering across Databricks, ADF, Synapse demonstrates transferable depth
